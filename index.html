How it works

Write something today that you want to receive years from now. We store it securely and deliver it to you on the date you choose.

01

02

04
Receive Your message arrives when its time comes.

Built to last.

No accounts. No passwords. No complicated interface. Just a message, a date, and enough infrastructure to keep it safe.

RULES: READ BEFORE EMAILING

• One active capsule per email address.

Each sender email address can have only one active capsule at a time.

• One additional recipient.

You may send a capsule to yourself and one additional recipient. The same recipient may receive multiple capsules from different senders.

• Maximum message size: 10,000 characters.

The capsule message must contain 10,000 characters or fewer.

• Plain text only. No attachments.

The capsule message must be plain text. Attachments are not accepted.

• Use ISO 8601 format for the delivery date and time.

The subject must follow this exact format: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM+05:30 or YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM+05:30|recipient@email.com. Any other format is invalid.

• Delivery must be more than one calendar year away.

Capsules scheduled for one calendar year or less from the time they are stored are invalid.

• The capsule clock starts when it is stored.

The delivery period is calculated from the exact date and time the capsule is successfully stored on the server.

• Capsules over 100 years are not accepted.

A delivery date more than 100 calendar years from the storage time is invalid. For custom arrangements and pricing, contact contact@since2026.in.

• Extra recovery information for long-term capsules.

For capsules scheduled more than a 10-calendar-year period ahead (3,652 or 3,653 days, depending on the dates), you may add a phone number and postal address at the bottom of the message using: {phone:+91XXXXXXXXXX} {address:Full postal address}

• Email delivery comes first.

Phone or postal delivery is used only when the corresponding recipient email is unreachable when the capsule becomes due.

• Partial delivery failure is handled individually.

If one recipient's email is unreachable but another recipient's email is reachable, fallback delivery is attempted only for the unreachable recipient.

• Fallback delivery is available only for long-term capsules.

Text-message or Indian postal delivery may be attempted only for capsules scheduled beyond the 10-calendar-year period and only when the relevant email cannot be reached.

• If all delivery methods fail, the capsule is kept safe.

Failed capsules remain stored for an additional 10 years after their scheduled delivery date.

• Your Capsule ID is your recovery key.

If all delivery methods fail, contact contact@since2026.in with the Capsule ID from your confirmation email and a valid email address. The Capsule ID should be kept private.

• A confirmation email seals the capsule.

Once you receive the acceptance confirmation containing your Capsule ID, the capsule cannot be edited, cancelled, or replaced.

• No confirmation means no sealed capsule.

If your submission follows all requirements but you do not receive a confirmation email, contact contact@since2026.in.

• Custom capsules are possible.

For messages over 10,000 characters or other special requirements, contact contact@since2026.in.

• Rejected submissions do not generate emails.

Invalid submissions are rejected without a confirmation email.
