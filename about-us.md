# About Since 2026

Since 2026 is a simple idea:

Write something today. Choose a day in the future. Let time take care of the rest.

We built this as a small digital time capsule for the things that matter to us — messages to our future selves, words meant for someone we love, feelings we want to preserve, and questions we are willing to wait years to answer.

There are things we say immediately.

There are things we wish we had said.

And there are things that are more meaningful when they are allowed to wait.

Since 2026 exists for those things.

## Why did we create it?

The honest answer is very simple.

We wanted to ask one question:

**Did she marry him?**

The idea came from a lighthearted moment in a publicly shared video. A little girl confidently talked about a boy she loved, saying that she was going to marry him someday.

It was innocent, funny, and strangely memorable.

The thought was simple: instead of wondering what the answer might be twenty years from now, why not create a time capsule and ask the question properly when the time comes?

That small idea became Since 2026.

This isn't about following someone's private life, predicting what someone will do, or expecting an answer.

It's simply about preserving a harmless question inspired by a public moment and giving time the chance to answer it.

And from that one question came a bigger idea:

**What if anyone could preserve a message today and let their future self discover it years from now?**

That's Since 2026.

## What it is

Since 2026 is a deliberately minimalist digital time-capsule service.

There are no accounts to maintain, no complicated interface, and no social feed.

You send a message, choose when it should arrive, and we preserve it until that moment.

The goal is not to make the process complicated.

The goal is to make the waiting meaningful.

## What you can put in a capsule

A capsule can be anything you want your future self or someone you love to receive later.

A letter.

A confession.

A promise.

A memory.

A question.

A reminder of who you were.

Or simply a few words you don't want time to erase.

## Built for the long wait

Since 2026 is designed around a simple principle:

**The message should outlive the moment in which it was written.**

The project is intentionally small and uses simple, portable technologies wherever possible.

The project is also open source. The public repository contains the blueprint and documentation needed to understand how Since 2026 works and how it can be rebuilt in the future.

[View the project on GitHub](https://github.com/divyam-gawde/since2026)

## One message. One date. A lot of time.

You don't have to know who you'll be years from now.

You don't have to know what life will look like.

Just write something that matters today.

Seal it.

And let time do the magic.
